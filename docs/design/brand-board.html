07Imagery and voice

Warm, private, never fearful

Inherited from the Dua guidelines, tuned for women's health.

✓Do

  • Portrait at eye level, soft smile, white coat, plain warm background.
  • Mothers, newborns and families, with written consent.
  • Short, kind sentences. Always end with a next step.
  • Talk about privacy: female doctor, one-to-one room.
"Most period pain can be treated. A short visit helps you understand yours."

✕Don't

  • Stock models, ultrasounds of identifiable patients, or anything graphic.
  • Fear-led breast cancer messages or guaranteed outcomes.
  • Pink everywhere: rose is an accent, ribbon pink is seasonal.
  • Two primary buttons side by side, or rose as the main button.
"Don't ignore this lump or it could KILL you! Book NOW!!"
